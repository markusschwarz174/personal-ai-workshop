# Experience Store

The experience store keeps reviewed lessons that may improve future work. It is deliberately smaller than run history.

## Admission test

Keep a lesson only when all four questions can be answered:

1. What happened in a real run?
2. What evidence supports the lesson?
3. Where might it be reused?
4. What limitation prevents overgeneralization?

The human approves admission. A model may propose a lesson but must not silently promote it into workshop memory.

## Do not store

- entire chat transcripts;
- temporary status;
- unverified model opinions;
- private source material;
- a rule inferred from one ambiguous outcome.
